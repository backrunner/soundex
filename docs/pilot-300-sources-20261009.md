## Integrity and quality

The official FLAC archives passed full CRC checks. All 23 retained originals
match the exact archive member bytes and recorded SHA-256 values. Full-file
decoding and signal inspection found no unresolved quality flags. All 300
selected originals were rebound to their retained audio hashes before launch.
Alternate versions, channels and codec variants do not multiply the work count.
Native lossless publication and a clean decode do not prove that every upstream
production step avoided lossy audio; known lossy exports are excluded.

Representative tracks from both new creators passed 12 MP3, AAC and Vorbis
encode/decode alignment cases at 44.1 and 48 kHz, with zero residual sample
offset and finite derived channel roles. These checks supplement the full
preparation and manifest audit; they are not a new model-quality result.

After member integrity and retained hashes were verified, the two redundant
ZIP files were removed, saving **791,330,981 bytes**. Originals, publisher
pages, grants, credits and audit receipts remain local and ignored by Git.

## Pilot collection

The reviewed pool now has **300 works / 22 creators / 1,212.39 music minutes**.
VCTK contributes a separate **23.85 minutes**, giving **1,236.24 source minutes**
across future training, validation and test splits. Slakh contributes zero.

Reviewed repertoire counts are China 29, Japan 48, Korea 58, Europe 32,
North America 95 and unknown 38. Genres are ambient 30, blues 11, classical 55,
country 5, electronic 56, folk 29, funk 2, hip-hop 2, jazz 7, pop 14, rock 59
and unlabeled 30. Regional attribution describes creator repertoire, not
nationality or comprehensive regional-market coverage.

The [300-work policy](dataset-curation.md) has **zero blocking gaps** and
**13 advisory coverage gaps**. R&B and Chinese/Japanese/Korean vocal music
remain absent; country, jazz and hip-hop still need more creator diversity.
The new collection meets the first-run policy, rather than the stricter
1,000-work acquisition plan.

The fresh run freezes committed source and selected recordings. It verifies
the matching VCTK supplement, prepares music codec pairs and audits them before
starting model optimization from random weights. Speech sampling is derived
from the smallest actual prepared music genre. Preparation must not be reported
as already completed model training; local `progress.json` is the live status.

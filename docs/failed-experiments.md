# Failed and unqualified experiments

This is the mandatory lookup before a new experiment. It records rejected routes,
unqualified candidates and inconclusive diagnostics, not a claim that every related
architecture is intrinsically ineffective. The linked reports retain exact budgets,
seeds, metrics, counterexamples and local evidence paths. Raw local reports and
frozen checkpoints remain available; they are not public training-data artifacts.

The current default weights are unchanged. P38 completed the detector-conditioned
backbone comparison; both output paths failed their registered screen. P39–P43 are gradient, update-transfer and timing diagnostics. Later entries
distinguish adapter training, analytic interventions and calibration explicitly;
see the latest status in the table and [research index](research.md).

## How to use this record

[P63](band-training-p63.md) starts six registered fresh global/parameter-matched/band
trajectories, 16,384 updates each; the first global control is running after all
six preflights, with no completed quality comparison yet. Its
first launcher rejects an order-only catalog mismatch before training; v2
preserves byte-identical draws and adds the regression test. A separate fixed
8,000-hop CPU/wake audit retains 18 band and two global late completions, including
one band stage wall time above 5 ms (24.131125 ms, 1.108583 ms thread CPU).
Off-CPU delay remains a real deployment failure, not an exemption or a reason
to replace the original P62 timing record.

[P62](band-tcn-p62.md) implements the band TCN and passes numerical/native
parity plus the compute screen (maximum 1.218834 ms). Its paced case still fails
continuity: 2/1,000 completions late, maximum 3.622951 ms. Retain the state-size
preflight rejection and all measured failures. Zero training updates: this is
feasibility evidence, not improved audio or a qualified deployment model.

Completed bounded diagnostic: [P61](critic-objectives-p61.md) retains 768 gradient
records and 1,280 PCM probes. Structured-negative critics have prediction-uphill
saved-Adam directions on 15/16 fit batches in both origins. Every learned arm
loses to random features in source-equal mean MR probe benefit, with seven music
MR counterexamples and five feature-step regressions retained. Retire this
classifier recipe as the next auxiliary; no claim against all GAN/feature methods.
The [architecture review](architecture-evaluation-p61.md) proposes the matched
band-token causal comparison now prototyped in P62. No threshold change or promotion.

Completed follow-up: [P60](texture-context-p60.md) separates observed-band
summaries and structured negatives. All eight 1,024-update endpoints finish;
all four arms fail the prerequisite. Structured negatives fix phase/time-control
ranking but reverse actual prediction ranking to 3-4/45 music and 0/16 speech
sources preferring the reference. Context alone does not fix phase recognition.
Diagnose component gradients before considering separate task outputs; do not
retune mixture weights on exposed calibration or repeat unchanged training.
No restoration generator or deployment weight changes.

1. Find the closest previous route and read its report before changing training.
2. Name the unresolved hypothesis and the changed control, representation, data
   exposure or deployment mechanism. Do not repeat an unchanged rejected setup.
3. Freeze endpoints, budget and assessment rules before scoring. Preserve every
   seed and every measured run, including failures and invalid preflights.
4. Add the outcome here and in a detailed report: endpoint hashes/evidence paths,
   comparator, failed criterion, counterexamples, absolute and relative errors,
   runtime failures, limitations and the condition that would justify revisiting.
5. A corrected measurement may supersede an invalid measurement, but must not erase
   it. A diagnosis is not a quality-qualified model. Reused development recordings
   remain rechecks; fitted crops cannot establish cross-recording generalization.

Status meanings: **not qualified** means an overall restoration/runtime screen was
not met; **diagnostic** means the experiment cannot establish deployable quality;
**invalid measurement** means the measurement needs correction before interpretation.
A positive submetric is retained even when the candidate is not qualified.

## Evidence retention and future entries

Each linked report is the authoritative detailed record for its experiment, including
per-seed and per-recording failures. This index consolidates routes rather than
inventing missing measurements. Local `data/runs/` registrations, assessments,
raw native reports and checkpoint receipts remain the auditable evidence. Cleanup
must preserve them. If an old report lacks an independent-test or listening result,
that evidence is missing; it must not be inferred from the index.

or update here with: status; exact comparison and budget; failed rule and metric;
all counterexamples; relative and absolute magnitude where relevant; runtime and
parity status; local evidence/endpoint hashes; what remains untested; and a concrete
reason a future repeat could answer a different question.
